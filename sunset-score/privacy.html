Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Sunset Score is a free Android app by N.Z. Apps.

  • Location: sent to Open-Meteo for the weather, and to Google to show your city name.
  • Google sign-in (optional): your name and email stay on your phone.
  • Photos and settings: stay on your phone (and in your own Google backup, if it's on).
  • No ads, no tracking, no selling of data. Not aimed at children under 13.
  • Delete everything: uninstall the app.
